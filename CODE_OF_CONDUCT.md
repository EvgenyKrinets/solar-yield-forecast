# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's home, energy or account data.
- Accept correction gracefully and help others learn.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information and sustained disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
